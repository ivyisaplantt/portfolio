Photography

    
I first got into photography almost two years ago when I was gifted a digital camera. Since then, I've enjoyed capturing the world around me, particularly nature, architecture, and the small details that might go unnoticed. Photography has become a way for me to slow down, appreciate my surroundings, and preserve moments that I find beautiful or interesting. I tend to gravitate toward landscapes, buildings, and scenery rather than portraits, and this gallery is a curated collection of some of my favorite photographs.

    
I chose photography as an artifact because I wanted my portfolio to offer a glimpse into who I am outside of academics and software engineering. I hope it gives readers an opportunity to connect with me on a more personal level, whether that's through shared interests in traveling, exploring new places, or simply appreciating a good photograph. Beyond being a creative outlet, photography has also helped me develop an appreciation for composition and attention to detail. I think these qualities carry over into my technical work, particularly when thinking about how people interact with and experience the products I help build. Ultimately, this gallery represents a side of me that isn't necessarily captured by a resume: someone who is curious about the world and likes sharing those experiences with others.
